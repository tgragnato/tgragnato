Year after year, the [OSDay](https://osday.dev) just keeps getting better! On April 24th, [Schrödinger Hat](https://schroedinger-hat.org) brought us together at [The Social Hub Firenze Belfiore](https://www.thesocialhub.co/it/firenze-belfiore/) for another incredible edition. It was a fantastic day packed with inspiring talks, great conversations, and top-tier open-source energy.

- `Francesco Corti` Open Source Won the War, but Is Losing the Peace: A Call for New Governance Models
- `PJ Hagerty` Why Git Still Matters
- `Luca Del Puppo` AI in the Browser: Am I Still Waiting for It?
- `Serena Sensini` YOLO at Sea: Real-time AI for Ship and Wreck Detection
- `Soumaya Erradi` From Code to Things
- `Maciek Palmowski` Build Once, Switch Anytime, CMS Freedom with Astro Collections
- `Diego Braga` Open Source in Production: Why Your Company Needs an OSPO (Before It's Too Late)
- `Ivan Pesenti` OCR Magic in Go: Build AI-Powered Text Extraction with Ollama in Minutes
- `Michela Bertaina & Alessio Fattorini` Community vs AI: Dueling to the Last Spell
- `Laura Trotta` OpenPAIN: The Unexpected Struggles of Maintaining an Open Source Library
- `Maurizio Argoneto` From Code Smells to Strategy Spells: Boosting Maintainability
- `Emiliano Pisu` Kinetic CSS: A Deep Dive Into Modern Motion APIs
- `Mario Fiore Vitale` Data Pipeline Troubleshooting: Root Cause Analysis With Lineage Metadata
- `Lorenzo Spinelli` The Human in the Loop
- `Valeria Salis` Demystifying Green IT: The Most Common Misconceptions About Sustainability and Technology

Thanks to all the people who made the event possible!
